# Bodies Meeting the World: Edge Effects As the Crane Flies by Jin-me Yoon considers the complex co-existence of North and South Korea through film of an old military ground near the DMZ. She uses a thermal camera, just as South Korean military surveillance and border security do. Placed inside a small room, a “bunker,” you are bombarded by sound... outside the room, the sound bleeds, relating to edge effects by mimicking how politics between borders interact. In addition to (as mentioned on her website) sonic meditation and warfare, Yoon focuses on the power of sound to propose new futures. The DMZ is incredibly biodiverse, a reminder that the borders created by war are permeable. Within earshot of Yoon’s bunker are three lively drawings. In Chambers’ selected pieces by Fawkes, thick black lines define nudes thrumming with color. Her drawings are held within white frames on a white wall, just as her drawn bodies are contained by their edges. Chambers speaks of “vectors of movement” and “bodily gestures across time and space” to describe her selections. Formal choices Fawkes made that reinforce those ideas are how her colors scribble in bound tangles of energy, and divide the body according to imagined light. From Muybridge to Duchamp, artists continually strive to represent the duration of experience; to collapse time and space into one image. If edge effects are when two communities meet, then the two communities meeting in Fawkes’ works are the body and the space it interacts with, or movement meeting space, or the body meeting art. Although mounted in a traditional museum format, the piece then forwards interaction: our body meets this body of art. Chambers/Fawkes and Yoon both use medium to capture life (color, thermal cameras). Crucially, they both think about the movement of a body traversing space. Yoon focuses on bodies crossing borders, while Chambers thinks more broadly about bodies through space. In terms of “edge effects”, Chambers considers the meeting of the body with the world. Yoon represents two countries interacting, as well as the past meeting the present and the future through sonic transformations.
