## Untitled Fish Series 2024, pastel. Images I was working on in 2024, in which I meld fish with childhood drawings. I like the idea of anatomically correct realistic fish and scribbles—don’t know that I ever fully achieved my goals though. There’s a separation between the two still. *“Piranha”, 2024, oil pastel.* Piranha was inspired by drawings I made as a child. *“Nurse Shark”, 2024, oil pastel.* For the nurse shark, I met a shark in a cylindrical cage in an aquarium that I was obsessed with. It just swam in loose endless circles. The way the lights fell across it was really beautiful and yet I was sorrowful that it was reduced to just circling. In my drawing, I wanted to capture the motion and the light in a kind of ode to this shark.
