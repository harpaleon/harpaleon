## Figure Drawings ![later portrait, 2025](../art/portrait.jpg width=297)![first figure drawing, 2025](../art/figure025.jpg width=330) Over the years I've attended various figure drawing classes and sessions, and created things of varying quality. My growth and lapses as an artist are visible in the results. ![highlights, 2025](../art/figure1325.jpg width=330) My favorite supplies to use are: - kneaded eraser (used sparingly) - chalk pastels (in three shades) - chalk pastel pencil (dark) - paper (not picky) I still use pencil sometimes, but orangey chalk pastels are the best. I've also experimented with white pastel or erasing sections for highlights. I've found adding these lighter areas suddenly brings life to muscle. I improved visibly on proportions once I discovered [comparative measurement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comparative_measurement) (which I had to write the Wikipedia article for?). ![proportion struggles, 2025](../art/figure225.jpg)![10 minutes, 2025](../art/figure325.jpg) ![practicing faces and accuracy to life](../art/figure1025.jpg) ![january 2025](../art/figure625.jpg) ![feb 2025](../art/figure525.jpg)![march 2025](../art/figure725.jpg) ![20 min, april 6, 2025](../art/figure125.jpg) ![15 min, april 2025](../art/figure1225.jpg) ![april 2025](../art/figure1425.jpg) ![20 min, april 2025, using highlights](../art/figure1325.jpg) ![5 min, april 2025](../art/figure825.jpg) ![april 2025](../art/figure1525.jpg) ![5 min, happy with gestural quality](../art/figure425.jpg) ![5-15 min, love the softness](../art/figure925.jpg)
