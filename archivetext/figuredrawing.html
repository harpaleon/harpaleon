## Figure Drawings Over the years I've attended various figure drawing classes and sessions, and created things of varying quality. My growth and lapses as an artist are visible in the results. ![later portrait, 2025](../art/portrait.jpg width=297)![first figure drawing, 2025](../art/figure025.jpg width=330) My favorite supplies to use are: - kneaded eraser (used sparingly) - chalk pastels (in three shades) - chalk pastel pencil (dark) - paper (not picky) I still use pencil sometimes, but orangey chalk pastels are the best. I've also experimented with white pastel or erasing sections for highlights. I've found adding these lighter areas suddenly brings life to muscle. ![example of highlights](../art/figure1325.jpg width=330) I enjoy figure drawing because it offers such drastic areas for growth. I improved visibly on proportions once I discovered comparative measurement. Although I don't use it much when drawing informally (outside of classes), I hope I still retain some of it intuitively. I feel more confident now when sketching objects' relations to each other by eyeballing them. I still want to work more on faces, and what to do with poses lying down or crouching. I find my proportions get a little stretched, or I get bored, or don't know what to do with the pose. I did the most figure drawing in 2025. After a lapse of over half of year, in 2026, I tried to get back into it.
